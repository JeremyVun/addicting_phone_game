## Full description

Settle is a calm block puzzle for adults. Place three shapes at a time on an 8x8 grid. Complete a row or column to clear it, and make consecutive clears to build a combo. A game ends when no remaining shape fits.

Choose how you play:

- Classic mode is an endless high-score game.
- The daily puzzle gives everyone the same puzzle each day and tracks your streak.
- Earn coins and raise your player level as you play.
- Collect 12 colour themes and 16 achievements.

Settle is designed for short portrait sessions and one-thumb play. There is no account or sign-in. Your progress stays on your device.

The game works offline. An internet connection is needed for ads and purchases. Rewarded video ads are optional and can provide a continue, new pieces, double coins or a second daily attempt. Occasional full-screen ads may appear between games.

In-app purchases include coin packs, all themes and Remove ads. Remove ads also provides one free continue in each game.
