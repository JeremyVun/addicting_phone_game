### What the app collects

### Ads

Settle uses Google AdMob to show rewarded video ads and occasional full-screen ads. AdMob may collect the advertising ID, coarse location derived from the device’s IP address, device information, and other device identifiers. Google may use this information to provide, measure and improve advertising.

In the EEA and UK, Google’s consent form asks you to choose how ads may use your data. You can change this choice later in Settle’s Settings.

Read [Google’s Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the [AdMob partner information page](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/9012903) for more information.

### Purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play Billing. Settle never receives or stores your payment details. Google processes purchase information under its own privacy policy.

### Analytics

Settle may send anonymous gameplay events to [developer name]’s own analytics server. For example, an event may record that a game ended and include a score range.

These events use a random identifier that is not linked to an account or personal data. The identifier resets if you uninstall and reinstall the app.

### Notifications

Settle can show a local daily reminder if you allow notifications. The reminder is scheduled and shown by your device. No personal data is needed to provide it.

### Data on your device

Game progress, settings, coins, themes, achievements and streak information are stored only on your device. There is no cloud account or cloud backup provided by Settle. Removing the app may remove this data.
